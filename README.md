# Ecommerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that visualizes **Ecommerce sales performance** across multiple categories, products, and regions.  
The goal of this project is to identify **key revenue drivers**, **high-performing products**, and **business trends** to help make informed decisions.

---

###  Key Highlights
- **Total Revenue:** ₹1.25B  
- **Average Order Value:** ₹112.85K  
- **Cancellation Rate:** 30%  
- **Lost Revenue due to Cancellations:** ₹525.42M  
- **Total Orders:** 15.69K  

---

##  Insights Gained

###  Revenue by Product
- **MacBook Air** and **OnePlus 9** lead sales, generating ₹145M and ₹143M respectively.  
- **Top-selling categories**: Laptop and Mobile contribute majorly to total revenue.

###  Revenue by Category
- **Laptops (₹390M)** and **Mobiles (₹188M)** dominate sales.  
- Accessories and Chargers show potential for growth with mid-level performance.

###  Revenue by State
- **Maharashtra (₹247M)** and **Gujarat (₹239M)** are top-performing states.  
- States like **Telangana** and **Delhi** show lower revenue contribution — opportunities for marketing expansion.

###  Revenue Trend by Month
- Strongest months: **July (₹134M)** and **March (₹126M)**.  
- Drop in **November**, possibly due to seasonal shifts or limited promotions.

---

##  Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Excel / CSV Data Source**
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
- **Power Query Editor** for data cleaning and transformation

---

##  Data Cleaning & Preparation
- Removed null or invalid entries.  
- Standardized product and category names.  
- Created calculated columns and measures using DAX for KPIs like revenue and average order value.  
- Built slicers for **Product Category** and **Date Range** to enable easy filtering.

---

##  Dashboard Features
- Interactive **slicers and filters** for dynamic analysis.  
- Visual comparison across **products**, **categories**, and **states**.  
- Monthly trend visualization using line charts.  
- KPI cards with key metrics at a glance.  
- Responsive and presentation-friendly layout.
